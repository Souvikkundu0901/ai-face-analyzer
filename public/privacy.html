Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2026 • Version 0.5.0

🛡️ Our Core Zero-Retention Promise

We do not store your face photos. Uploaded and webcam selfies are processed entirely in temporary, volatile server memory and are immediately and permanently destroyed once numerical metrics are extracted. No images are ever saved to disk, cloud buckets, or persistent databases.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. Depending on whether you use the service anonymously or as a registered user, we collect only the following:

  • Ephemeral Image Payloads (Anonymous & Registered): Your selfie photo is held in volatile RAM only for the duration of the computer vision analysis (typically <150ms) and discarded immediately. It is never persisted.
  • Account Credentials (Registered Users Only): Your email address and a securely salted bcrypt hash of your password. We never see or store your plaintext password.
  • Derived Analysis Metrics (Registered Users Only): If you choose to persist scans in your account, we store only anonymized numerical metrics (geometric aspect ratios, facial shape classification, color-space lightness/redness indices, spot coordinates, and timestamps).
  • Technical Metadata: Request timestamps, response status codes, and IP addresses in volatile memory for rate-limiting protection. IP logs are automatically rotated and purged.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for the following functional purposes:

  • To execute facial landmark detection and skin surface analysis requested by you.
  • To display longitudinal progress comparisons and metric deltas across your previous scans when signed in.
  • To enforce rate limiting on our API to protect against denial-of-service and credential stuffing attacks.
  • We never sell, lease, rent, or trade your personal information or derived metrics to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

3. Third-Party Integrations

Our service integrates with select technical infrastructure under strict privacy parameters:

  • Google Gemini API (LLM Explanation Layer): If AI explanations are enabled, the service transmits only approved, deterministic observation rule IDs and non-personally-identifiable numerical scores (e.g. REDNESS_MODERATE, redness: 0.35). No image files, names, or email addresses are ever transmitted to the LLM.
  • Error Tracking (Sentry): Application exception monitoring operates with send_default_pii = False. Diagnostic crash logs never include image bytes or user payload content.

4. Your Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

You have full, autonomous control over your data at all times:

  • Individual Scan Deletion: You can permanently delete any scan record from your History tab at any time. This triggers an immediate, physical database row deletion.
  • Complete Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account from Account Settings. This initiates a complete, irreversible cascading deletion across all your personal records, scans, and session tokens. There are zero soft-delete flags or shadow backups.
  • Right to Export: You can print or save your analysis report as a PDF directly from the interface at any time.

5. Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard defenses to protect your session and data integrity:

  • In-memory sliding-window rate limiting on all authentication and analysis endpoints.
  • Cryptographically hashed (SHA-256) single-use refresh token rotation with immediate reuse/theft detection.
  • Strict binary magic-byte inspection on file uploads to prevent extension spoofing and malformed payload injection.
  • Encrypted HTTPS communication in transit with strict Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS) enforcement.
